# Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

Status: 🟢

Architecture Decision Record (or ADR for short) is well-established format of documenting architecture decisions. ADR is a human- and AI-friendly format. It allows product teams to effortlessly document decisions taken during a lifetime of the project.

ADRs can be created on various level of abstractions depending on the given organization's and team's needs, but our personal favorite is documenting:
- Context
- Decision
- Consequences (Pros & Cons)
- Alternatives Considered

We recommend adopting ADR for virtually every software project. Well deserved green status.
